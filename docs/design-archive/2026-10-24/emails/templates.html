Sign-in email templates

The six sign-in emails for the Other Half, in the order Supabase lists them. Each has its subject and body ready to copy.

Once
  1. In Supabase, open Authentication → Emails → SMTP Settings, set Sender name to The Other Half, and click Save.
Then for each email
  1. In Supabase, open Authentication → Emails, then the template with the same name.
  2. Copy the subject here and paste it into Subject.
  3. Copy the body here, click into Supabase’s Body box, press Cmd+A, then Cmd+V.
  4. Click Save, then tick it off here.

The curly-brace bits, like {{ .ConfirmationURL }}, are Supabase’s placeholders. Leave them exactly as they are, and Supabase fills in each person’s link or code.
