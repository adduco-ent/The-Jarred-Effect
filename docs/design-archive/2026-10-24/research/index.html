The winners

Who won, and why it worked.

Each card says what the award recognized, the growth evidence where it exists, and what it means for the Other Half.

Tiimo

iPhone App of the Year 2025

Apple: it turned “chaotic calendars into a timeline of soothing colors” and made “tackling our to-dos a calming activity.” Its site claims over half a million people.

For us: calm is a feature. Make the hard part, today’s step, feel like relief.

Seven patterns

What the winners have in common.

  • Calm the hard part

    The best apps make the scary step feel soothing: Tiimo’s colors, Calm’s atmosphere. For you, that’s the moment someone opens today’s step.

  • One signature visual

    Lumy’s sky, Tiimo’s color timeline, Structured’s day line. People remember one picture, and it becomes the brand.

  • Something to care for

    Finch’s bird, Focus Friend’s bean, Bears’ characters. Care brings people back, and care gets shared.

  • Progress that forgives

    Gentler Streak and Finch never punish a miss. Hard days still count, which is already your rule.

  • Show people themselves

    How We Feel and Opal turn taps into patterns people recognize. That is self-discovery made visible.

  • Earn the ask

    Noom’s onboarding runs up to 113 screens and asks for money only after people have invested in their own answers.

  • Proof up top

    Tiimo, Opal and Finch put ratings, awards and outcomes right under the headline, before any feature.

What this means for the Other Half, whichever direction you pick

  • Test asking for a card when someone starts their free week, with a clear reminder before the first charge, against today’s no-card week. The benchmark gap is about 5×, though asking up front also filters out less committed people.
  • Make day 0 count: finish step one right after the quiz, so the first win lands before the first doubt.
  • Keep the quiz, and make its result the “aha.” Noom shows that a personal flow earns the ask; yours already does this in three minutes.
  • Front-load the welcome emails. Calm got 4× the revenue from the same emails sent closer together.
  • Collect proof now: a rating, real quotes and one outcome number from testers, for the top of the app page.
  • Keep the copy at a grade 5–7 reading level, which your voice rules already push toward.
Direction A

Sky

From Lumy, Tiimo and Calm.

Your day, in its own light. The app already greets people with a sky that follows their clock. Make that light the whole interface, from the page that sells it to the last step of the day.

The app

  • Today sits on the live sky for their time of day; steps are soft frosted cards.
  • Finishing a step moves the sun along its arc, so progress is something you see.
  • Each day done becomes a star; the week draws a constellation. A missed day stays a dim star, never a broken chain.
  • The paywall shows their week’s constellation: “Keep your sky going.”

The app page

  • The hero shows the visitor’s own sky by their local time: morning, afternoon, evening or night.
  • One button, the trust row, and two phones at dawn and dusk.
  • A scroll from morning to night tells how a day with the app goes.
  • You, then questions, then the ending under the night sky.

Why it converts

CalmSignatureIt lowers the stress of starting, the way Tiimo did, and the constellation is a picture people want to keep and share. The sky also bridges site and app, so the hand-off feels like one place.

Watch out for

Beauty can’t slow clarity, and the night needs strong contrast. It needs crafted motion to feel premium. Effort: medium to high, though the skies already exist in the app.

Companion

Something to take care of. Your other half becomes a small two-tone being, half light and half shadow, that grows brighter and more balanced as you do your steps. You stay the voice; it is the face.

The app

  • Home is the companion; each step done lights its other half, and a small version on a hard day still counts.
  • A gentle glow streak: a missed day dims it, rest days are allowed, nothing dies.
  • A weekly ritual with it, milestone marks to collect, and share cards.
  • Your words stay the voice of every message.

The app page

  • A playful hero with the companion breathing on screen: “Meet your other half.”
  • One button, a short page, ratings once testers rate it.
  • Built to be shared: the companion is the ad.

Why it converts

ReturnSharingAttachment brings people back daily. Duolingo’s streaks and leaderboards came first in its 4.5× growth, Focus Friend hit No. 1 within a month, and Finch is estimated near $1M a month.

Watch out for

It can read young or game-like next to your mentor positioning, and it lives or dies on the quality of the character art and animation. It must never guilt-trip. Effort: high.

Direction C

Mirror

From How We Feel, Opal, Structured and Tiimo.

See yourself clearly. The product is self-discovery, so make the person the hero: a living balance map of their two halves that shifts with what they actually do, and insights in plain words that ask “Right?”

The app

  • Home is their balance map across your four pairs, starting from the quiz and moving week by week with their steps and check-ins.
  • “What I’m noticing” becomes a daily card with real numbers and “Right?” / “Not quite.”
  • A weekly mirror that compares them only to their past self.

The app page

  • A product-led page in the Tiimo and Opal mold: “See which half you’ve been missing” and the quiz button.
  • A proof strip, then a live example balance map.
  • Three feature rows with real screens, outcome numbers from testers, questions, the ending.

Why it converts

ClarityAhaThe “aha” arrives in three minutes, because the quiz result is the first mirror. It scans fast, it is the easiest to test and improve, and it is self-discovery made visible.

Watch out for

Data can feel clinical, so it needs your warmth in every line, and it deepens only after a few weeks of use. The quiz map seeds it on day one. Effort: medium.

My pick

Sky, with Mirror inside it.

Sky gives the Other Half a look nobody else in this space owns, and it’s already in your app. Mirror gives people the reason to pay: they watch themselves change. One idea comes from Companion: the gentle streak, drawn as a constellation instead of a character.

  1. The experience is the sky. The app page, Today and the welcome all live in the light of the visitor’s day.
  2. The value is the mirror. The balance map and “What I’m noticing” sit at the center of Progress and the paywall.
  3. The habit is the constellation. Days done become stars, misses stay dim stars, never a broken chain.
  4. The page converts on proof. Ratings, quotes and outcome numbers from your testers go right under the headline.
