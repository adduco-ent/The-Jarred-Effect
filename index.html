What is the Other Half?

An app that turns the other half into a few minutes a day. You take a free 3-minute quiz, and I build your first week from your answers: one small step a day for the goal you care about, with a smaller version for hard days. You check in after each step, and the next ones adjust to you.

How much does it cost?

Your first week is free, and you don’t need a card to start. After that it’s a simple monthly membership, and you’ll see the price before you’re asked for anything.

Is it written by AI?

Partly, and I’d rather tell you straight. The method and the voice are mine. AI writes your steps from them, fresh for your goal and your life, and every step is checked against rules I wrote.

Is this just another vision-board / manifestation course?

No. Vision boards and affirmations are one pole of the work — the “ask” half. This covers the half most courses skip: the discipline, friction, and shadow work that make the “ask” actually land in real life.

Do I need to already believe in manifestation for this to make sense?

No. Call it energy, psychology, or just how people change, and it works the same way. You don’t have to believe anything first: you try one small step, see what happens, and let your own results do the convincing.

What if this doesn’t feel right for me?

Then trust your gut. You know your life better than any method, mine included. Follow your intuition and watch how it plays out. If things go sideways, look back at where they turned, without blaming yourself. That’s data too, and over time it shows you when your gut is real intuition and when it’s fear in disguise.

How long is the free guide?

About a 10-minute read. It’s built to be used, not just read — most of it is things you can test the same day.

Will I get sold to constantly after I sign up?

You’ll hear from me when there’s something worth saying: new guides, news about the Other Half, occasional real talk. No daily hype emails, no fake countdown timers.
